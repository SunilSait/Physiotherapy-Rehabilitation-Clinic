FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Learn more about our clinical methods, insurance coverage, and what to expect during your first therapy visit.

Under Direct Access legislation, you do not need a physician's prescription or referral to schedule an initial consultation and begin physical therapy at RestoraCare. If your specific health insurance policy requires a referral for billing claims, our dedicated intake coordinators will coordinate directly with your primary physician to obtain proper documentation seamlessly.

Your first appointment is a 60-minute comprehensive one-on-one diagnostic assessment with a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy. We perform functional movement screening, muscle strength testing, joint mobility tests, and posture evaluation. You will also receive your initial hands-on therapeutic treatment and a customized recovery plan with clear milestone goals.

Most patients experience noticeable pain reduction and enhanced mobility within their first 2 to 3 sessions. Acute muscle strains typically resolve in 6 to 8 sessions over 3 to 4 weeks, whereas complex post-surgical rehab or chronic spinal disc issues generally benefit from 12 to 16 sessions. We re-evaluate your progress at every visit to ensure rapid, measurable results.

Yes, RestoraCare is proudly in-network with over 30 leading private insurance carriers, including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, and major workers' compensation administrators. Our patient coordinators verify your exact benefits and co-pays prior to your initial visit so you encounter no hidden surprises.

Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting workout clothing (such as gym shorts, t-shirt, or athletic leggings) and supportive athletic footwear that allows your therapist to inspect and manipulate the affected joint easily. Bring your photo ID, health insurance card, and any relevant MRI or surgical notes if available.
